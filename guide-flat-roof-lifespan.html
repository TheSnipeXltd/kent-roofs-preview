How long does a flat roof last?

There is no single answer. It depends on the system, the quality of the installation and how well the roof drains.

Draft guide. Tom to check it for accuracy before publishing.

4 minute read

Felt

Felt is a common choice on extensions and garages. Newer high-performance felts last longer than older types. Joints and edges are where it usually fails first.

Fibre glass

Fibre glass (GRP) forms one seamless layer, so there are no joints to open up. It suits awkward shapes and can be trimmed neatly at edges and upstands.

Rubber

Rubber membranes come in large sheets with few seams and are known for long service life when laid well.

What shortens a flat roof's life

Standing water, poor falls, blocked outlets, damage from foot traffic and badly sealed upstands or rooflights. Regular clearing of leaves and a look after storms help.

Typical lifespans

Manufacturers and trade bodies publish ranges for each system, and installers give guarantees. Ask for the figure and guarantee for the exact system in your quote.
