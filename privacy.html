What we collect

  • What you send us when you ask for a quote: your name, phone number, postcode, the services you want, and anything you add about the job, including photos you send.

The website has no account area and no analytics at present. It does not collect your details itself: the quote form builds a message that opens in WhatsApp on your device, and nothing is sent until you press send there.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a quote.
  • To carry out the work, arrange access and keep you updated.
  • To keep accounts and records the law requires.
  • To deal with complaints, claims or guarantee queries.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

We use your information to take steps at your request before a contract and to perform it, to meet legal obligations such as tax records, and for our legitimate interests in running and protecting our business.
