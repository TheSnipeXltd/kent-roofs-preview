Signs your roof needs attention

Many roof problems are small when you catch them. Here is what to look for, and what to leave to a roofer.

Draft guide. Tom to check it for accuracy before publishing.

3 minute read

From the ground and inside

Slipped, cracked or missing tiles or slates; sagging lines on the ridge; damp patches or brown stains on ceilings; daylight in the loft; and water marks around chimneys and roof windows. Binoculars help.

Around the edges

Gutters overflowing, plants growing from them, and sagging or rotten fascias and soffits. These can let water into the roof edge.

Lead and flashings

Lifting or cracked lead around chimneys, walls and dormers is a common route for leaks.

After a storm

Check for debris, displaced tiles and new stains. Take photos if you can do so safely from the ground.

What not to do

Do not climb onto the roof or lean ladders against fragile edges. A roofer should inspect it. Moss on its own is rarely an emergency, but a lot of it can hold moisture.

When to call

Any active leak, missing tiles or storm damage is worth a call. Send photos and your postcode on WhatsApp and we will tell you what we see.
